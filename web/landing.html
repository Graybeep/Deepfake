The part most demos skip

What this will not tell you.

A detector that hides its failure modes is worse than no detector, because people act on it. These are on the page, not in a footnote.

A score is not a probability

No temperature has been fitted. A 62 is not "62% likely fake" — it is an uncalibrated model output.

Not robust to adversarial input

Scores are manipulable by deliberate perturbation. No pre-classifier is built. Documented, not hidden.

Not production-validated

The weights are a public research checkpoint that has never been validated against this pipeline. The bands are unmeasured against it.

Compression looks like manipulation

Resampled and recompressed images score in the same range as spliced ones. See the screenshot row above.

"Couldn't decode" is not "no face found"

Two different answers with two different advisories. An unreadable upload means not analysed, never nothing there.

Not a compliance claim

Media is deleted on completion and the audit row is retained, which is meaningfully better than nothing — and is not a legal determination this codebase gets to make.
